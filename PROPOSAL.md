## 1. Project Overview
The proposed Aircon Service Management System aims to automate and organize the daily operations of the company’s multiple branches. Currently, processes such as booking, scheduling, billing, and progress tracking are handled manually through Messenger, Excel, and handwritten records.

This project will replace manual methods with a centralized online system accessible through a web browser.

---

## 2. Objectives
- Simplify and automate the booking process from different sources (partner stores, phone calls, Messenger).
- Provide accurate technician scheduling and tracking across branches.
- Reduce billing errors and improve payment monitoring.
- Store and manage customer information digitally.
- Generate real-time sales, service, and performance reports.

---

## 3. System Users
- **Admin**: Full access to all branches, reports, and management functions.
- **Branch Leader**: Limited access to branch-specific bookings, schedules, and reports.

---

## 4. Key Features

### A. Booking Management
- Record bookings from multiple sources (partner stores, Messenger, phone).
- Assign technicians to jobs per branch and availability.
- Display job status (Pending, Ongoing, Completed).

### B. Customer Records
- Store customer contact details, service history, and branch information.
- Allow quick search and retrieval of past transactions.

### C. Technician Scheduling
- View technician availability.
- Create and update schedules per branch.

### D. Billing and Payments
- Record transactions (cash, e-wallet, bank transfer, cheque).
- Generate invoices and monitor pending payments.

### E. Reports
- Daily, weekly, and monthly service summaries.
- Sales performance reports per branch.
- Technician performance tracking.

---

## 5. Technical Specifications
- **Platform**: Web-based system (desktop and mobile browser accessible).
- **Database**: Centralized online database for all branches.
- **User Access**: Role-based login (Admin, Branch Leader).
- **Hosting**: Cloud-hosted or local server.

---

## 6. Expected Benefits
- Eliminates manual errors and duplicate records.
- Provides faster and more accurate billing.
- Improves technician management and scheduling efficiency.
- Enables real-time data visibility across branches.
- Reduces workload on admin and improves customer satisfaction.
